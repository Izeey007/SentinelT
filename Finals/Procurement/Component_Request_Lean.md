## What was removed/deferred to save money

The following are **not requested in the reduced first purchase**:

- three ST3215 hammer/torso servos;
- serial-bus servo controller;
- servo extension kits;
- LM35 temperature sensor;
- buzzer;
- full 2m x 1m HDPE sheet;
- full 6m mild-steel bars;
- extra XT60 lead/connector quantities;
- separate XT60 charger cable;
- 6mm shaft collars that did not match the 5mm cutter shaft concept.

The hammer and powered torso mechanisms are deferred. They can be added only if Kyle confirms spare budget after the drive, RC, safety and cutter systems are funded.

## Material/fabrication cost strategy

Do **not** buy the full HDPE sheet and full steel bars before the lab cut list exists. Ask TUT first for:

1. suitable HDPE/HMWPE offcuts or cut-to-size panel material;
2. aluminium plate/angle offcuts for the main chassis;
3. small real-steel offcuts for the pointed front part and rear cutter;
4. workshop cutting/drilling/machining using existing lab equipment where available.

Target the lab material/fabrication addition at roughly **R1,000-R1,500** if suitable stock/offcuts and lab labour are available. If not, use the actual lab quote rather than silently increasing the online request.
