## Before final ordering

1. Confirm the final Robo Wars technical rulebook and allowed mechanisms.
2. Confirm whether the physical build must stay below the project's 5kg engineering target or can use the public 10kg event limit.
3. Get TUT workshop approval for the cutter shaft, bearings, coupling, steel grade, guard and retention.
4. Confirm a wheel replacement if the listed Communica set remains unavailable.
5. Confirm the contactor/relay's DC interruption rating and no-auto-restart logic.
6. Verify final branch fuse/current ratings after motor current measurements.
7. For the hammer/torso package, obtain stock/alternatives and re-run the mass budget before purchase.
8. Re-run the digital footprint audit after replacing CAD parts with the actual supplier dimensions.
9. Weigh the finished robot and verify the physical 500 x 500mm starting footprint.
