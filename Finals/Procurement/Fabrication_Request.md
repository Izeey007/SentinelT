## Material and process decisions

- Cosmetic metallic finishes are not structural steel. Select materials by their documented properties, installed mass and role.
- The submitted mild-steel bars are provisional raw-stock entries, not a specification for finished impact parts or cutters. Do not purchase full bar/sheet quantities until a cut/nesting list is approved.
- Request suitable offcuts or cut-to-size stock when economical. Include grade, thickness, quantity, drawing revision and price on each quote.
- The workshop should select cutting, drilling, machining and joining processes for the actual material. Welding is not assumed necessary everywhere; accessible bolted assemblies can simplify maintenance.
- Do not assume a steel edge will cut an opponent's metal, or that a servo torque label establishes hammer durability. Those outcomes are not established by the current design evidence.
- No fabrication drawings, weld specifications or active-mechanism performance claims are released by this request.
