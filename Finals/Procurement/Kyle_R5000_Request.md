The remaining allowance is intentionally left for delivery charges or supplier price changes.

## Systems covered by this request

The funded components support the complete SentinelT concept:

- four-wheel RC drivetrain;
- 2.4 GHz six-channel control;
- powered steel spinner/cutter;
- two powered hammer mechanisms;
- powered upper-body/torso rotation;
- emergency-stop control;
- reduced-mass 3S battery system.

The three 40kg.cm servos replace the previously selected out-of-stock ST3215 servos. They are currently listed at 60g each and use standard PWM control, reducing both cost and installed mass.

## Sourced separately

The following items remain required for assembly but are not included in the R5,000 funding request:

- motor drivers;
- Raspberry Pi Pico W;
- main battery isolator and relay/contactor;
- fuse holders and fuses;
- servo power regulator;
- battery charger and LiPo safety bag;
- XT60/Deans connectors and adapters;
- wiring, terminals, heatshrink and cable management;
- bearings, shaft collar and coupler;
- structural bolts, washers, nyloc nuts and threaded inserts;
- aluminium/HDPE/steel stock, motor mounts, wheel hubs, guards, hammer arms and spinner/cutter material.

These will be sourced separately or fabricated from suitable available material.

## Mass-control requirement

The completed robot must remain below **5.0 kg**. Fabrication should target approximately **4.50-4.60 kg** to preserve a practical inspection margin. The 2200mAh battery is approximately **143g**, the three selected servos are approximately **180g total**, and the lightweight 100mm wheels are used to reduce drivetrain mass.

Final compliance will be confirmed by weighing the fully assembled robot before competition.
